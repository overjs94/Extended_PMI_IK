THE RESEARCH QUESTION

From a QP to a monotone VI.

Body-local virtual inertia and sparse joint couplings expose the maximal-coordinate structure. A skew velocity-product operator contributes zero power while changing the transient solver flow.

D = 2G/Δt + B + αJᵀKJΔt + C,   Cᵀ = −C

What this build measures

Reduced PMI, maximal QP-PMI and retained-skew VI, with centralized, Schur, QP ADMM and QP/VI DRS options. The NumPy reference is serial. ADMM/DRS additionally offer compiled CPU worker threads. Measured speedups are specific to the workload and worker count.

Scope & provenance

Based on the supplied RAL paper, maximal-coordinate white note and PMI-IK source. Single Panda uses the supplied URDF; multi-arm assemblies are synthetic. Rigid grasps are virtual constraints. Obstacle avoidance uses explicit sphere proxies with distance inequalities and optional receding waypoint preview; full-mesh collision checking, GPU execution and full ROS executable reproduction remain open.
